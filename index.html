      How these fares are calculated

      Uber, Bolt and FREENOW don’t offer open price APIs, so Taryfa estimates each fare from a pricing model per service (base fare, per km, per minute, minimum fare), the real driving route between your two points, and live time-of-day demand – surge for app rides at rush hour and weekend nights, and the night/Sunday tariff for licensed taxis. The price shown in the app at booking is final.
